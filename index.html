Keep Your Goodies Fluffy

How to Care for Your Crochet 🧼🌸

🫧

Gentle Cold Hand Wash

Submerge in lukewarm or cold water with a drop of gentle baby soap. Do not wring or scrub harshly.

🧺

Lay Flat to Dry

Pat out excess moisture with a clean towel. Lay flat in shade to preserve stitch tension and vibrant hues.

✨

Gently Fluff & Reshape

Once completely dry, gently pinch and roll amigurumi or ruffles with clean fingers to restore full fluffiness.
